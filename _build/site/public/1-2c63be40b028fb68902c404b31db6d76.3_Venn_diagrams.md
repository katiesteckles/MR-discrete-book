# Venn Diagrams

John Venn was born in Hull, England in 1834. In 1853 he went to Gonville and Caius
College Cambridge and graduated in 1857 becoming fellow of the college. For the next 5
years he went into priesthood and returned to Cambridge in 1862 to teach logic and
probability theory.
John Venn is popular known as the person who developed a graphically way to look at sets
and this graph become known as a Venn diagram. The sets were represented by oval or
circular shape figures but they can be any shape.
The properties and relations of sets can be displayed with Venn diagrams. The diagrams use a
rectangle whose interior points are assumed to represent the elements of the universal set U:
