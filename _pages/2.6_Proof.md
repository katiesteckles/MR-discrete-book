derive a contradiction.
Example
Prove that 2 is the only even prime.
Proof:
2 is prime (factors of 2 are 1 and 2).
Assume p is an even prime  2.
Since p is even, then 2 divides p.
Therefore p is not prime.
The assumption produces a contradiction and must be false so there is no even prime
greater than 2. Hence, 2 is the only even prime.

The most common types of proofs are ones where we wish to establish the truth of a
proposition of the form (P  Q). For propositional logic there are several different
proof methods. For example, using the rules of natural deduction or the formulae of
semantic tableau. The latter is a method based on showing that the expressions are
tautologies (or contradictions) by examining the structure of the expression using various
truth formulae called axioms.
